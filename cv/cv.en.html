How I work with AI

Mindcoding (my term), not vibe coding: AI writes a lot of the code, I make the decisions. Together with the agent I write the project rules, a project map and docs; tasks live in a prioritised pool, and the agent takes them one at a time or in batches of up to 10, checks its work against the architecture and sends the result to me for review. A task that would take a junior/middle developer a day takes minutes of agent time plus my review. Tools: Claude Code, Codex, Greta — my own agent for MetaForge.
